Questions

Asked and answered.

Basics

What exactly is Buzzer?
A browser app that controls your OSSM sex machine, and layers an AI companion (face + voice + personality), story-driven scenes, multi-person avatars, live sessions with friends, and toy add-ons on top.
Is it free?
Yes — the app is free and runs entirely in your browser. AI features can use the free Pollinations endpoint, or your own API key (DeepSeek, OpenAI-compatible, Gemini) for privacy and quality. ElevenLabs voices use your own key.
What do I need to run it?
A Chrome- or Edge-based browser (Web Bluetooth is required for the OSSM), an OSSM, and Bluetooth. No install, no account, no server.
Does it work on iPhone?
The interface works everywhere, but Web Bluetooth for the OSSM needs Chrome or Edge. On iOS, Web Bluetooth support is limited — use a Chromium browser where available, or a desktop/Android device.

Safety

How does safety work?
A safe word stops everything instantly. The machine never starts until you've confirmed your minimum/maximum depth and maximum speed. The AI can only raise limits with your confirmation (or in Wild / Full-submission mode, which you choose). Guests keep their own limits and safe word on their own device.
Can the AI hurt me?
The AI stays within your confirmed depth and speed limits and respects your safe word. Fisting and e-stim add-ons include their own extra safety guidance. You remain in control at all times.

AI & voices

Which AI providers are supported?
DeepSeek, OpenAI (and any OpenAI-compatible server: Groq, Together, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio), Gemini, and a free Pollinations endpoint. Each provider keeps its own API key.
Is the free AI private?
No — Pollinations is a shared, unauthenticated public endpoint. For private conversations use a keyed provider. DeepSeek processes data in China.
What voices can it speak in?
Your device's built-in voice, ElevenLabs (your key), Google, VoiceRSS or Fish. Each character in a multi-person cast can have a different voice.
Does it remember me?
Yes — it stores a memory of what you share (name, tastes, preferences) locally. "Forget me" erases it while keeping your settings and keys.

Scenes, casts & sessions

Do I need to write scenes?
No. Scenes are optional structure. Without one, the companion improvises freely — including full multi-person threesomes from a cast.
What is a "cast"?
A set of 2+ characters, each with their own look, voice and linked toy. The AI plays all of them, switching who speaks and whose toy moves.
Can the AI control two machines differently?
Yes. With a cast, each character can target a different toy (your machine, a guest's, or an add-on), so one can thrust hard while another does something gentle — in the same moment.
How do sessions work?
One person hosts and shares a code; others join peer-to-peer (WebRTC). You get private chat, optional webcams, video sharing, and a room AI. There's no relay server — connections are direct and encrypted.
Is a guest automatically "played" when they join?
Never. A guest is a spectator until they complete their own private "get ready" flow (connect, safe word, limits) and opt in. The room AI can't touch their toy before that.

Privacy

Where is my data stored?
On your device. Chats, memory, settings, keys and media live in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to Buzzer (there is no Buzzer server).
Are my API keys exported?
No. Exports (settings, backups, avatars, scenes) never include your AI or voice API keys. The settings export is obfuscated so keys can't be read by simply opening it.
Can others see my chats?
Only people you invite into a live session, and only over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection. There is no central storage.

Troubleshooting

It won't connect to my OSSM
Use Chrome/Edge, enable Bluetooth, and make sure the device isn't paired to another tab. Reload and tap Connect.
The machine won't start
Confirm your limits (min/max depth, max speed) and safe word first — Buzzer won't start until you have.
My avatar disappeared / reset
Very large photos can overflow storage — use reasonable sizes. Export your avatar regularly as a backup.
The AI's suggestion chips are stale
They're generated per reply and fall back to sensible defaults. If they look stuck, start a new session (↻) to reset the conversation.
