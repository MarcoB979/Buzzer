The possibilities

One app.
Everything your machine can feel like.

A quick tour of what Buzzer adds on top of full OSSM control.

Control

Three ways to drive

Advanced Penetration — fine-grained depth, in/out speed, acceleration and time modifiers for a stroke that breathes.

Standard — simple speed / stroke / sensation / pattern for everyday use.

Funscript — load a funscript and let the machine follow the action beat-for-beat.

Companion

An AI that plays you

A companion with a face (drawn or your own photo/video, animated with blinks and lip-sync), a voice, and a memory. It learns your name, your tastes and your words — then drives the machine as the person you want it to be.

Scenes

Story-driven scenarios

Scenes give a session a storyline: floating visuals, timed beats, trigger words, strictness (free → strict), and toy triggers. The AI follows the script, shows the right moments, and fires the right toy at the right time — or improvises if you want freedom.

Multi-person

Two avatars, two voices, two toys

Build a cast of characters, each with their own look, voice and linked toy. The AI plays both, speaks in both voices, and can act on each toy independently — one fucks you while the other does something else, in sync.

Sessions

Play with friends

Host a room, share a code, and friends join peer-to-peer. Private chat, optional webcams, video sharing, and the room AI can drive everyone's toy — in sync, or each person separately. Guests get their own private "get ready" flow before anything moves.

Add-ons

Fist · Ejaculate · e-stim · more

Fist-IT for fisting play, Ejaculate for watersports scenes, Coyote with 72 curated e-stim wave patterns, and any Buttplug.io toy through Intiface — all controllable by you or the AI, with per-addon safety rules.

Video

Watch together, in sync

Play local folders, online favorites, or a Stash library, cast to a TV with Chromecast, and keep the machine's motion synced to the video.

Voice

Talk and listen

Speak to your companion hands-free and hear it answer back — via your device's voice, ElevenLabs, Google, VoiceRSS or Fish. Each character in a cast can have its own voice.

Safety

Your limits always win

A safe word stops everything instantly. Depth and speed limits must be confirmed before the machine starts, and the AI can only raise them with your consent (or in modes you explicitly allow). Guests keep their own limits and safe word on their own device.

Privacy

Yours, on your device

No server, no account, no tracking. Chats, memory, keys and media stay local. Sessions are encrypted peer-to-peer. Your AI keys are never included in exports.
