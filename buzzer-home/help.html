Everything, explained clearly.

From first connection to full multi-person sessions — follow along in order, or jump to what you need.

1 · Getting started

You need: a Chrome- or Edge-based browser (on phone, tablet or PC), an OSSM in range, and Bluetooth switched on. Everything runs in the browser — no app store, no account.
  1. Open Buzzer. The interface loads instantly and works offline once open.
  2. Tap 🔌 Connect and pick your OSSM from the list. The status dot turns green when paired.
  3. Pick a mode at the top: Advanced Penetration (full control), Standard (simpler), or Funscript.
  4. Move the sliders, or press Start and adjust as you go. You're driving.

2 · Safety first

Buzzer never lets the machine start before you've told it your limits. Do this once per session:

  1. Tell the companion a safe word (anything you'll remember and can say instantly).
  2. Set your minimum depth, maximum depth and maximum speed — either type the three numbers, or let the companion guide you by feel (it slides in slowly; you say "stop" at each point).
  3. Confirm. Only then can the machine move on its own.
The safe word always wins. Saying it stops everything immediately, no matter what mode you're in or who is controlling. A common everyday word (like "stop") can fire by accident in chat — Buzzer will gently suggest a more distinctive one.

3 · AI companion

  1. Open 💞 AI Companion. On first run it walks you through a short setup: what to call it, what you're into, and how it should look.
  2. Use the 🧑 Builder to change its look — drawn, or your own photos/videos with animated blinks, expressions and body states.
  3. Set its persona (who it is) and your About yourself (who you are). It uses both to stay in character and pick the right dynamic.
  4. Pick an initiative level — 🛡 Strict (asks first), Playful, Wild, or Full submission (it may raise limits without asking).

Then just talk. It remembers what you share, and it can drive the machine whenever you ask — or, at higher initiative, on its own.

4 · Voice & talk

Tap 🎤 Talk to speak a line, or 🎧 for hands-free conversation while playing. Replies can be spoken aloud:

  • Device voice — no setup, works offline.
  • ElevenLabs — paste your own API key + a Voice ID for realistic voices.
  • Google / VoiceRSS / Fish — other providers you can pick in AI settings.

Each character in a multi-person cast has its own voice — set it in the avatar builder's 🗣 Voice section.

5 · Scenes

A scene is a story the AI role-plays with you, with optional structure:

  1. Open 🎬 Scene and create one, or start from a bundled sample.
  2. Write the scenario in your own words. You can add [scene:name] beats, [timer: after 3-5 minutes] events, and [toy:eject start …] triggers inline.
  3. Set strictness: 0 = free improvisation, 100 = follow the script closely.

Don't want a story? Skip scenes entirely — the companion improvises on its own.

6 · Multi-person cast

Want two partners at once? Build a cast:

  1. In the avatar builder, create each character as its own avatar (name, persona, look, voice).
  2. Tap ＋ Add current avatar to cast for each one.
  3. For each cast member, set its toy — host (your machine), a guest's name, or an add-on like coyoteA.

With 2+ characters, the companion shows them side by side, speaks as both, and can drive each toy independently. Saving a scene also saves the cast; selecting it restores the characters.

7 · Live sessions

Invite a friend (or friends) into your session:

  1. Open Sessions and tap ▶ Host a session. Share the code or link.
  2. The guest enters the code and taps Join. You'll both appear in the room with private chat.
  3. Control: you decide who may control your machine; mirroring lets a guest copy your machine's motion.
  4. Playing together: the room AI chats with everyone and can drive each person's toy. Guests are never touched until they've cleared their own safety setup in a private "get ready" room.
Multi-person sessions: with a cast of two avatars, one character can stay with you while the second accompanies the guest into their private setup — then you all play together.

8 · Add-ons

Enable add-ons in Settings → Add-ons:

  • Fist-IT — fisting play with its own safety guidance.
  • Ejaculate — watersports (piss) scenes.
  • Coyote (DG-Labs) — e-stim with 72 curated wave patterns across two channels.
  • Buttplug.io — any Buttplug-compatible toy through Intiface.

The AI can drive all of them through the addon field, and scene markers like [toy:…] can trigger them at the right moment.

9 · Video & cast

  • Local videos — open a folder and the AI can play any file by name.
  • Online favorites — save URLs and play them inside the app.
  • Stash — connect your Stash server to browse its library.
  • Cast to TV — send video to a Chromecast and keep the machine in sync.

10 · AI providers & keys

In AI settings, choose a provider and add its key. Each provider keeps its own key:

  • DeepSeek — private, keyed; processes data in China.
  • OpenAI (compatible) — works with OpenAI, Groq, Together, OpenRouter, or a local model (Ollama / LM Studio) via a base URL.
  • Gemini — Google's model.
  • Free (Pollinations) — no key, but a shared public endpoint with no privacy guarantee.
Your keys stay on your device and are never included in any export. The free endpoint is not private — for anything personal, use a keyed provider.

11 · Backup & share

  • Backup (Settings) — saves everything (settings, avatars, scenes, memory, media) as a file you can restore.
  • Settings export — obfuscated file with your keys; import on another device.
  • Avatar / Scene export — share a single avatar or scene (including its voice and cast) as a zip.
  • Forget me — erases what the AI learned about you while keeping your settings and keys.

12 · Troubleshooting

It won't connect to my OSSM
Use Chrome or Edge, enable Bluetooth, and make sure the OSSM isn't already paired to another tab or device. Reload and tap Connect again.
No sound / voice doesn't speak
Check the 🔊 toggle is on and your device isn't muted. ElevenLabs needs both a key and a Voice ID — use the Test button.
The machine won't start
Buzzer requires confirmed limits (min/max depth, max speed) and a safe word before it will start the machine. Tell the companion your numbers.
A guest can't be controlled by the AI
The guest must finish their private "get ready" flow (connect, safe word, limits) and be marked ✅ ready before the room AI may drive their toy.
Camera / microphone is blocked
Tap the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → allow Camera/Microphone, then reload.
