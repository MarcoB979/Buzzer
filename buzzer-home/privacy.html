Privacy

Private by design.
Honest about what leaves your device.

The short version: Buzzer has no server and no accounts. Your chats, memory, settings, keys and media live on your own device. A few features connect to third-party services (AI, voices, live sessions) — each one is listed below, and each one is optional.

1 · What stays on your device

These are stored only in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, and never uploaded to Buzzer (there is no Buzzer server):

  • Your settings and API keys (AI provider keys, ElevenLabs key, Stash key).
  • The companion's memory of you and your About yourself.
  • Your avatars, scenes, casts, and any photos or videos you add.
  • Your chat history and session logs.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it. "Forget me" in Settings erases what the AI learned while keeping your settings and keys.

2 · Exports never include your keys

When you export settings, backups, avatars or scenes, your API keys are never included. The settings export is obfuscated so a key can't be read by simply opening the file.

3 · What is sent to third parties

Buzzer is a client-side app: some features talk directly to external services from your browser. Use them only if you're comfortable with that service's own privacy policy.

AI providers

When you use the AI, your conversation text is sent to the provider you selected — DeepSeek, OpenAI (or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure), Gemini, or the free Pollinations service. Each has its own policy:

  • DeepSeek — processes data in China.
  • OpenAI / compatible — see that provider's policy; a local model (Ollama / LM Studio) stays entirely on your machine.
  • Free (Pollinations) — a shared, unauthenticated public endpoint with no privacy guarantee. Never use it for anything personal.

Voice (text-to-speech)

Spoken replies are sent as text to your chosen voice provider — your device's built-in voice (offline), ElevenLabs, Google, VoiceRSS or Fish. ElevenLabs and the others use your own key and their own policies.

Live sessions (peer-to-peer)

Sessions connect people directly (WebRTC) — chat, video and control messages travel between participants, not through Buzzer. Connection setup uses the free, public PeerJS signaling service, which sees only connection metadata (peer IDs), not message content. Only invite people you trust.

Social features (optional)

If you use the Friends / presence / messaging features, they connect to a shared Supabase project (public, anonymous access): your display name, friend code, friend connections, presence and messages. You can use Buzzer fully without ever enabling this. This is the one feature that is not peer-to-peer.

Other small integrations

  • Avatar images — the drawn avatars are generated by DiceBear (api.dicebear.com); only a seed and colors are requested.
  • Web search — the companion's "explain this term" feature queries Wikipedia (opensearch).
  • Chromecast — casting loads Google's Cast SDK and connects to your TV.
  • Libraries — hls.js, mp4box, PeerJS, JSZip and Supabase are loaded from public CDNs.

4 · Bluetooth

Buzzer uses Web Bluetooth to talk to your OSSM and add-ons locally. Pairing happens in your browser; the app never transmits device data anywhere.

5 · Cookies & tracking

Buzzer sets no tracking cookies and shows no ads. It has no analytics. We cannot and do not sell, share or profile your data — because we never receive it.

6 · Your choices

  • Use a keyed AI provider (or a local model) instead of the free endpoint.
  • Use the device voice instead of a cloud voice.
  • Only invite people you trust into sessions.
  • Skip the social feature entirely.
  • Clear the site's data or use Forget me at any time.

7 · Adult content & age

Buzzer is an adult product. It contains no pornography itself — it connects to your own devices, your own media, and AI conversation you direct. By using it you confirm you are 18 or older in your jurisdiction, and that you are solely responsible for the rights to any photos or videos you add and for everyone who appears in or uses them.
